# 2026-11-23 · P24 offset routes around blocked turns

**English** | [中文](updates-2026-11-23-p24.zh-CN.md)

[Source commit 258eeff](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/commit/258eeff3a462089af427507745250f497ce88411) · [Source PR #5, awaiting merge](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/pull/5) · [Source acceptance notes](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/blob/258eeff3a462089af427507745250f497ce88411/P24_DISASSEMBLY_DETOURS.md)

A board or tool can pass a blocked turn waypoint, leave the centerline before the obstruction, advance on a parallel offset, and turn in place. It can then rejoin diagonally or travel farther along the offset before making a lateral join. The search retains authored waypoints and later turns, and can also detour a separate straight obstruction.

- [a844111](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/commit/a844111306f9fbd4f09689e6e229ee3d1f9f42e3) allows a blocked waypoint turn to move onto a free part of the next leg.
- [9892cae](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/commit/9892cae09a00e5d5bd39f90de354bbccf4e7dc4c) lets a tool or board rejoin farther along the original leg after an offset turn.
- [258eeff](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/commit/258eeff3a462089af427507745250f497ce88411) allows departure, forward travel and the turn to occur at separate points. Joint search can handle an independent later straight obstruction.

## Verification

In the two-cabinet example, the tool leaves the second leg at 25%, moves 10 mm laterally, advances 55 mm, and turns 90° at the midpoint. The diagonal join adds 10.45 mm to the original route. With the diagonal blocked, it advances another 110 mm and rejoins laterally, adding 20 mm in total.

1,054 Node tests, 70 kitchen and cabinet Python tests, and 48 Edge workflows passed. Independent GEOS sampling checked 17,017 poses and reproduced the blocked turn and connectors. The two ZIP manifests covered 84 files. The DXF readback found 15 withdrawal centerlines, two native arcs, and zero CAD audit errors.

## Scope

This geometric check covers configured board, tool entry and withdrawal, and retained-tool transfer routes. It uses a bounded search and a 16-point, 50 m route limit. Manufacturer-specific assembly processes, fasteners, support and load checks, and CNC remain open.
