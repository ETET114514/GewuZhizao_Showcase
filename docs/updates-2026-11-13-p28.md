# 2026-11-13 · P28 manufacturing references and local procurement

**English** | [中文](updates-2026-11-13-p28.zh-CN.md)

Source [7714f04](https://github.com/ETET114514/GewuZhizaoNew/commit/7714f0486a557d35aa069b249a37d29947030ed6)

P28 now connects frozen quote comparisons, material/manufacturing review, reconstructed cabinet boards and manual concave boards, limited cover deductions, bounded stock cutting, source-bound manufacturing references, piece labels and local purchase/receipt/return records. Purchase prices are entered independently of selling prices. Full P28 remains unfinished; P24 whole-cabinet elevations and assembly detailing have not started.

- Frozen quote versions retain source revisions and SHA-256, settings reuse, per-item/specification comparisons and complete HTML/CSV/JSON differences independent of UI filtering.
- Material BOM confirmations expire when source geometry changes. Cabinet reconstruction and manual concave boards preserve through holes, top-face pockets, contour edging and depth-aware volume; circles use 64-sided geometry. CNY/USD/EUR/GBP/HKD use manually confirmed exchange rates and mixed tax/discount allocation.
- Cover/contact deductions are limited to supported cuboids; overlapping contributors are unioned once and geometry fingerprints invalidate stale confirmations. Unsupported shapes require review.
- Rectangular-blank guillotine planning compares up to 16 deterministic strategies within a fixed search budget, respects trim/kerf/rotation, and lists reusable/small offcuts. It does not guarantee the global optimum.
- Frozen manufacturing packs include A4 vector PDF, dimensioned SVG, millimeter DXF with native linear dimensions and depth/source XData, feature/directory CSV, geometry JSON, stock SVG, piece labels and a per-file hash manifest. These are manufacturing references, not controller-ready CNC.
- Local purchase drafts use frozen manufacturing demand; stock-sheet lines replace board-piece demand to prevent duplication within an order. Release, partial receive, return, re-receive and closure validate quantities, exact retry IDs and source state. Project history/save/reopen and complete CSV/HTML/ZIP exports retain source provenance.

The browser modules build reviewed geometry, pricing and stock snapshots; Python project storage normalizes frozen records and local procurement events. New-record validation independently recomputes supported geometry, stock and financial values. Read-only production export binds to the selected frozen revision/hash and uses explicit geometry-field whitelists: selling prices, taxes, customer fields and contract bodies are omitted from the manufacturing pack; entered names/model strings remain. The complete commercial snapshot remains a separate delivery export. Board drawings use local Y-up coordinates; stock SVG uses a top-left Y-down origin. Canceling export stops client waiting/download; read-only server generation may finish. Late responses cannot download into a different project.

1. In the detailing workspace, compare frozen quotes, then review material/manufacturing quantities, source confirmations, currency/tax settings and stock rules.
2. For a synthetic concave board, check through holes, pocket depth, edging and the rectangular blank. Compare the stock strategies and inspect kerf, trim, placements and offcut thresholds.
3. Freeze a source, use the local review/approval workflow and export its board-reference pack. Inspect PDF dimensions, DXF millimeter units/native dimensions, labels and hashes; do not send these references directly to a CNC controller.
4. Create a local purchase draft from the frozen manufacturing source. Enter supplier/reference and independent purchase prices; exclude nonpurchase services. Release locally, receive partially, return, re-receive and close. Export the source/event ZIP and printable HTML. This does not contact a supplier or payment system.

Remaining work includes arbitrary surface/solid deductions and external board reconstruction, multi-/back-face machining, manufacturer hardware/load checks, global irregular nesting and actual inventory, additional currency/tax rules, real identities/roles and shared approvals, online signatures, supplier ERP/payment and machine-specific CNC. Supplier/API/deployment, tooling and hardware documentation is still required for the external scope. Local approvals and entered actor names are not authenticated identities; the hash-linked event history is editable through project history and is not an immutable ledger.

Validation on 2026-11-13: **737 full Node tests and 70 related Python tests passed in the final pre-push rerun**; this is not a full Python-suite run. Earlier acceptance in this development batch passed the new procurement browser workflow and five existing P28 browser flows sequentially, including narrow layouts, plus independent ZIP/SHA-256, DXF, CSV and PDF read-back. These browser/export checks were not rerun for Showcase publication. Earlier counts overlap and must not be added. One earlier parallel browser startup failure was retained; subsequent sequential runs passed.

Independent read-back verified CRC/hashes for 11 manufacturing ZIP files, millimeter DXF audit and 600/400 native dimensions, a 0.17 m² profile and 6/12 mm pockets; piece M1#0 maps to sheet-1. The purchase ZIP verified three file hashes, a separate 120 CNY purchase against a 190 CNY selling quote, and six unique release/receipt/return/re-receipt/closure events. The actual printed purchase PDF has two landscape pages. A separate synthetic stock example improved from two sheets to one and purchase cost 200 to 100 while the independent selling quote stayed 300; this is not a general savings guarantee.
