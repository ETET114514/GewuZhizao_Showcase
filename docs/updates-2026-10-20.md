## Additions and fixes

- Panel seams/perimeter trim: regular grids, adjustable panel sizes/gaps, trim width/extra projection and independent materials; both layers preserve actual openings.
- Single-floor rectangular columns/beams/flues/enclosures: 2D click/drag, dimensions/height/elevation, 3D materials/shadows, copying, current collision and headroom beneath beams.
- Custom wardrobes/storage: boards, 1–6 proportional compartments, shelves/rails/internal drawers, open/single/double doors, per-compartment operation and part materials; placement, rotation, snapping and independent copying.
- Straight kitchen runs: base/wall presets, ordered cabinets of matching type/depth/height/elevation, retaining individual structures/materials/operation. Base runs share one independent-material worktop with thickness/front/end overhang settings.
- If individual edits break alignment, worktops pause with a realignment prompt. Cancel/undo restores them; ungrouping preserves cabinets. The first cabinet remains orthogonal; applying worktop edits realigns members.
- All four features share preview/cancel, undo/redo, recovery, save/reopen and high-resolution output. The source inventory distinguishes initial versions from remaining scope.

## Planned and unimplemented at this stage

Whole-kitchen movement/copying, L-shaped/irregular worktops, sink/hob holes and synchronization, more hardware, internal mechanisms/continuous-path collision, board schedules and construction drawings; opening surrounds, per-edge trim, irregular/recessed-light walls and floor origins/patterns; element integration with room area/openings, multiple floors and stairs. Flues/enclosures currently represent exterior envelopes; cabinet presets are not manufacturing cut lists.

Diagonal/curved walls, local wall heights, uploaded textures, persistent complete history, version comparison and cloud sync remain pending. Drawing/model exchange remains lower priority.
