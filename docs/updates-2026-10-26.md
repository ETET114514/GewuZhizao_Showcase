## Implemented capabilities

- **P13 N01–N03**: retains existing centerlines/corner segmentation, adds room-side net lengths, solid-wall segments excluding horizontal opening projections, room filters and withheld values for unconfirmed gaps. Switching measurement views does not change project data/history.
- **P22 E01–E03**: optional floor-contact deductions from paving/paveable area, with overlap unions and allocation across rooms. Shows gross/deducted/paveable area while retaining contours/per-piece records. Movement/copying/disabling/deletion restores coverage; consecutive element edits retain properties.
- **P25 D01–D03**: actual row/column subdivisions/mullions/transoms and per-cell operation, door transoms/sidelights; actual flat/stepped/beveled profiles and independent screens; rectangular three-sided bays, explicit collinear/orthogonal unequal-height connections and stepped frames retaining solid walls.
- **P24, three groups this round**: L-shaped wall/left-right blind-corner cabinets, U-shaped base/wall runs and continuous concave base worktops; rectangular/circular/rounded equipment holes and hole-only mode; ordinary/blind corners and two/three adjacent same-run cabinets, explicit shared-side-panel notches and internal-clearance checks.
- **P23 C01–C08**: free straight seams/simple polygons and individual wall/floor geometry/floor handles; composite feature walls/layers/recessed lighting, actual trim profiles/per-edge reveals/current-library frame fitting; more physical patterns/repeating motifs, custom skirting profiles and clipping to current cabinet occupancy. The agreed inventory scope is complete.

P13 centerline labels already existed; this round adds net measurements only. P23 closes only the chosen C01–C08 types. Completing a round does not implement every additional idea under the same identifier.

## Planned and unimplemented at this stage

Construction-dimension systems/finished-face clearances, complete construction drawings, model/CAD export and pricing; rotated/irregular structural elements and automatic wall/opening/region changes, multiple floors/stairs; product opening profiles/hardware, nonrectangular bays, folding/pocket/multi-track mechanisms and full motion paths; holes around corners/across more than three cabinets/free polygons, further kitchen mechanisms/product equipment/schedules/drawings; curved panels/profiles and extra product installation details remain in the inventory. P20 export remains lower priority.

Further multi-map materials, model compatibility, cloud collaboration, professional simulation and generative design are in [current limitations](features.md).
