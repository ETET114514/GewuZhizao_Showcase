## Implemented capabilities

- **P23 panel seams/trim**: wall-segment/floor origins, nonuniform horizontal/vertical rectangular divisions, per-edge rectangular width/projection/material/texture settings on perimeters/openings.
- **P23 per-opening exceptions**: each edge of a specific opening gets materials/uploads/dimensions; three inheritance levels—opening override, direction default, shared settings—with independent dimension/material restoration.
- **P23 opening reveals**: depth/board thickness, turning from finish face into actual opening boundaries up to wall centerline; no bottom for doors/full-height windows.
- **P23/P25 frame depth/clearance fitting**: single doors/fixed windows/single casements within one segment, with frame depth/inward-outward offset; frames/leaves fit lined clearance while structural openings remain. 3D shortcut opens the same 2D form.
- **P18 library/dedicated uploads**: search/rename/unused-entry deletion, protection for current project/history/saved versions/recovery; direct color uploads on panels/elements/kitchen worktops/procedural furniture/custom cabinet parts.

Fitting is off by default. Frames must lie within wall thickness; their depth's intersection with reveals determines the clearance envelope. Frames/leaves move together while structural openings/walls, regions and panels remain intact. Reveals stop at wall centerline, without bottom edges at doors/full-height windows. Preview/cancel, undo/redo, unchanged-edit redo retention, recovery and save/reopen share existing flows.

## Planned and unimplemented at this stage

- P23: per-panel selection/material/free editing, local seams/arbitrary polygons, nonrectangular profiles/bevels, irregular assemblies, per-opening/per-edge reveal depths, full-wall penetration, chevron/complex motifs, free floor-piece movement/actual geometry deletion, procurement/drawings.
- P25/P23: fitting across segments/connections/overlaps/unconfirmed gaps/corners, double/unequal/sliding/multi-leaf types, product profiles/installation details. Current fitting covers single doors/fixed/single casements within one segment, without enlarging structural holes or checking full motion paths.
- P18: separate normal/roughness/etc. uploads, batch management/cache cleanup, product light models, arbitrary GLB part materials and further batch replacements. Current color uploads do not generate PBR map sets.
- P16: box/multi-selection, nested/mixed groups and complete transport paths. Drawing/model exchange remains lower priority; see [features and limitations](features.md).

Twenty-three directions retain future scope: 15 have main workflows, eight lack stated target workflows although some foundations exist. Completing smaller items reduces gaps without marking all P18/P23/P25 planning complete or deriving a completion percentage.
