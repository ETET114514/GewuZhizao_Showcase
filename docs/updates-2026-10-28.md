## How to inspect

Select a room, open the complete-layout/path/motion panel, choose one room or all rooms, sample spacing and quarter turns, then generate. Compare separate metrics, preview and apply the whole chosen plan once. Fixed kitchens/custom cabinets/group members and ambiguous room assignments stay fixed. Add walking/carrying path points on the map or enter them; verify actual door passage and motion at current dimensions. Check openings from closed to their authored pose. Results distinguish clear, blocked and unproven.

Limits: at most 40 movable objects, 30 rooms, 3 plans; per-object 1600 sampled poses and 12 full recalculations. Complete generation does not mean global optimization or automatic route finding. Generic four-side checks do not infer a GLB's semantic front. Opening sweeps do not validate unknown hardware or motion beyond the authored endpoint. OCR belongs to P31; P25 additional mechanisms and accounts/cloud remain deferred.
